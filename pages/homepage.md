I am a theoretical astrophysicist studying dark matter in the local Universe, with a focus particularly on how dark matter is distributed in the Milky Way and its satellites. I apply computational tools to bridge between observation, astrophysical theory, and the particle physics of dark matter. 

I am in the final year of my PhD at Princeton University, where I work with [Mariangela Lisanti](https://phy.princeton.edu/people/mariangela-lisanti). Beyond my research, I am passionate about student-centric mentorship and nurturing scientific thinking, and I teach math in NJ prisons with the [Prison Teaching Initiative](https://mcgraw.princeton.edu/community-college-engagement/prison-teaching-initiative).
